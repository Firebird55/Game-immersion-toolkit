# 0.3.1

Phone lookup uses the available width and a scrollable sheet. Current line and
History use tighter spacing, with room for the audio player and accessible
controls on narrow screens.

PC capture restores verified ZZZ comic text without requiring a voice mapping.
Official Chinese/English bubbles remain in History after restarting, while
unavailable audio is labelled honestly. Diagonally overlapping bubbles and sound
effects no longer merge into one OCR group. Other dialogue retains its voice
mapping requirement.

Verification includes synthetic capture/storage/API regressions, desktop browser
events and reloads in Chromium/Firefox across phone, desktop and DPI viewports,
and the mobile layout/lookup suites in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. A local
native-OCR replay of the three reported frames verified progressive capture of
one, two and three unique official lines. User screenshots and game assets are
excluded from source and release artifacts.
