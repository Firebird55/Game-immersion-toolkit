# Game immersion toolkit 0.22.0

## More Steam games and original languages

New native readers cover **Spyro Reignited Trilogy**, **METAL GEAR SOLID V:
THE PHANTOM PAIN**, **METAL GEAR SOLID V: GROUND ZEROES**, **DEATH STRANDING**,
**A Plague Tale: Innocence**, **Detroit: Become Human**, **Portal**, **Portal 2**,
**Half-Life 2 and its bundled episodes**, **Persona 5 Royal**, and
**Deus Ex: Human Revolution – Director's Cut**. Automatic Steam discovery finds
their installed folders. Each reader exposes the official spoken languages,
including evidenced regional packs; missing packs remain selectable with setup
instructions instead of silently substituting another language's recordings.

**Original Half-Life currently supports authored text messages only.** Its
GoldSrc files have no official spoken subtitle track, and spoken dialogue
capture is not implemented. Setup and the support matrix state this limitation
instead of reporting it as a missing voice pack. It is distinct from Half-Life:
Source and Half-Life 2.

The [language matrix](../languages/en.md) and [per-game audits](../game-audits/README.md)
describe exact edition, campaign and language coverage. Installed recordings
were checked separately from absent-pack format support. A supported language
does not mean every dialogue format or every screen layout is fully covered.

## Easier reading during play

- **Current line stays visible after the subtitle disappears**, until another
  confirmed line replaces it. Game and session boundaries keep unrelated lines
  separate.
- Transcript headings use the actual source and translation languages. Dutch
  dialogue is no longer labelled Chinese by a fixed table heading.
- Cinematic and lyric pages retain separate caption identities. Capturing one
  page does not reveal the rest of the scene. Where exact audio cuts are not
  established, playback is labelled **full track** and the same recording is
  reused across captions.
- Longer floating dialogue and separate panels use stable, exact matching
  outside the calibrated subtitle lane. Short ambiguous labels, unknown UI and
  text shared with silent choices cannot borrow a spoken recording.
- The subtitle crop also rejects choice/UI collisions, including fuzzy matches
  and a spoken sentence embedded in an unvoiced page.
- Game languages beyond Lexirise's supported study languages remain readable
  as plain text. Greek original audio stays available without offering an
  unsupported account language or silently switching its reader to Chinese.

## Voiced and unvoiced dialogue stay separate

**Capture setup → Include unvoiced dialogue** enables confirmed silent choices,
messages and readable text. It is off by default. Each game's coverage report
separates those entries from missing recordings and unresolved text; a missing
voice never turns a line into supposedly silent dialogue.

The new readers inventory ordinary conversations, ambient speech, commentary,
cinematics, radio/tapes and narrative panels where their authored files establish
the binding. Unknown formats, missing packs and files changing during an update
produce actionable errors and retain the previous complete prepared table.

## Getting started and limits

Select the game's original voice language in Steam, finish any pack download,
then choose it in **Settings → Games & Languages** and prepare the table. Keep
game subtitles enabled. Use the capture preview to check your resolution and
unusual layouts; separate panels can appear beyond the lower subtitle crop.

Some runtime-selected voices, movie formats, texture-only writing and speech
without authored subtitles remain unresolved. The individual audits list these
gaps. File-level extraction, matching and representative original-audio decoding
do not establish a full playthrough or physical OCR/DPI acceptance for every
game. No game assets, transcripts or recordings are included in the release.

Regression tests stay in the repository and run in GitHub Actions. They cover
native format boundaries, language isolation, retained captions, unvoiced
selection and public support-matrix consistency; local probes focus on the
installed files that hosted CI cannot access.
