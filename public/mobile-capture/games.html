Prepare the languages you choose

  1. Download the Windows release, extract it and run Install.cmd.
  2. Open Settings → Games & Languages. Select your game, learning language and translation.
  3. Install the matching original voice in your game launcher, then prepare the selected table.
  4. Start the game with subtitles and check your capture area.

Lexirise is optional. Migaku works independently with a compatible browser extension.

What support means

Storefronts describe game languages. Reader support and expected support describe the toolkit. They do not promise that every subtitle or chapter has been tested.

Missing voice packs, missing official translations and ambiguous lines remain unavailable. Hito's World uses its existing text-only catalog.
